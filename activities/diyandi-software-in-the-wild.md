## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Attendees at Mugna, the festival ground with rides and stalls, who are unfamiliar with the layout — including repeat visitors, since the venue moved to Ludo Ground this year.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
The new venue makes the layout unfamiliar to almost everyone, even those who attended before.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
Find specific food, products, or rides within the festival ground easily.
---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
Large crowds and limited visibility make it hard to tell which stall or ride is where. Signage around the grounds is often unclear or missing, so even attendees who have been to Mugna before struggle to locate what they're looking for.
---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
A mobile app called Mugna OnSite. It has a simple map of the festival ground and a search bar to find stalls and rides. The map and stall list work offline, so users don't need mobile data. Showing current location is optional.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
It lets attendees search for a specific stall or ride and see where it is on the map, without depending on a data connection or getting lost in the crowd.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Search for the food, product, or ride they want to find.
2. Tap a result to see exactly where it is on the map.

---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: Works offline

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Mobile data is often limited or unavailable during the festival, so an offline app with a working search bar still helps people find what they need.

### Quality 2: Easy to use

**Why does this matter to users?**  
People unfamiliar with apps, especially older users, may struggle with a complicated one, so a simple design helps everyone use it without difficulty.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

Add a feedback option in the app, accessible once users have internet connection. Organizers can also invite comments on the festival's social media page, since people tend to comment on posts more than they leave app store reviews.
